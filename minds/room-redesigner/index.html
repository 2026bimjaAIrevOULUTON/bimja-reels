Before you start

Where does it run?

On Venice. You chat with it in your Venice account; drafts use free image models, and the HD final uses credits.

Will it move my walls?

No. With a photo it keeps your walls, windows and layout. Structural changes are only shown if you ask, with a note to check with an engineer.

How accurate is the budget?

It is indicative, built from typical rates for each band. Real quotes vary by city, vendor and finish.

Is my chat private?

Venice marks this Mind as Anon: the creator cannot see your chats.
